The task contract, autonomy tiers, bounded review policy, decision card, operational safeguards, and human attention measures are implementation proposals developed for this playbook. They are not presented as Microsoft requirements.

The playbook is a paraphrased adaptation and original operational synthesis prepared with AI assistance. Its page-level source map enables readers to distinguish inspiration from additions.

## Evidence limits

Source claims about returns, time savings, product availability, and perfect accuracy are not independently verified here and are not local performance results. Measure your own baseline, error rate, full costs, and human supervision burden.

Revisions should identify what was observed, how it was measured, what failed, and which claims remain hypotheses.
