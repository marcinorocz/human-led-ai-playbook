# Contributing

Write repository content, issues, and pull requests in English.

## Our scope

Contribute practical improvements to human agency, mental health as a design priority, boundaries, manageable communication, pauses, rest, privacy, and shared responsibility.

Technical implementation belongs with specialist projects such as [Cezar](https://github.com/open-mercato/cezar) and [Basal](https://github.com/rkinas/basal), or another appropriate tool. Refer implementation questions there; do not turn this repository into a competing runtime or deployment manual.

## Review criteria

- Does the change preserve mental health and dignity above productivity?
- Can a person decline, pause, and be unavailable without pressure?
- Does it reduce demands rather than add mandatory rituals?
- Does it preserve choice and avoid guilt, manipulation, or dependence?
- Does it avoid collecting or inferring unnecessary health information?
- Does it distinguish an operational practice from a clinical claim?
- Are sources attributed and limitations clear?

Do not submit diagnostic scoring, covert monitoring, productivity leaderboards, unsupported treatment claims, or practices that override a person's boundaries in the name of helping them.

## How to contribute

Open an issue describing the human problem, or submit a focused pull request. For a substantial change, explain the proposed behavior, trade-offs, and evidence. Clearly mark untested proposals.

Keep examples fictional or properly anonymized. Never post personal health histories, client data, credentials, or private conversations without appropriate permission. Participation and feedback are voluntary; do not pressure contributors to share lived experience.

Check links and wording. Preserve attribution. By submitting a contribution, you agree to make your original contribution available under the repository's MIT License. Include compatible permissions for third-party material.

Challenge ideas respectfully. A contributor may step away without explanation. No response-time commitment is implied.
